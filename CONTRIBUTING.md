# Help improve VerseOrder

VerseOrder is built for people who love Marvel stories. You don't need to be a developer to help make the watch order better.

If you think a movie, series, season, or special is missing—or that moving something would make the journey more satisfying—[open a suggestion](https://github.com/avizmarlon/verseorder/issues/new).

Tell us which title you're referring to, where you'd place it, and *why*. Links to reliable sources are especially helpful for release-date and continuity corrections. Please avoid revealing major story spoilers without a warning.

You can also report confusing navigation, accessibility issues, broken features, or problems with the viewing experience.

The final order is a curated recommendation rather than an official chronology, so thoughtful differences of opinion are welcome.
