# Interactive design explorations

These are **working prototypes**, not locked branding. Open `http://localhost:3000` and switch the top navigation to compare in the same data context. Captures (desktop and mobile): `docs/previews/archive-*`, `docs/previews/timeline-*`, `docs/previews/multiverse-*`.

## A. Cinematic Archive

Immersive opening, theatrical typography, abstract illuminated celestial art, title cards that expand on hover, streaming-like browsing, contextual detail and editorial progress.

**Hypothesis:** strongest instantly understandable landing page for new audiences.  
**Risk:** visual flair could obscure straightforward next-title navigation.

## B. Sacred Timeline

Luminous continuous spine, changing eras, checkpoints, alternating film cards, visible user progress and tactile sense of completing one epic journey.

**Hypothesis:** best core browsing experience for a single ordered narrative.  
**Risk:** 110 entries require careful virtualized/paged navigation, especially on mobile.

## C. Multiverse Explorer

Interactive orbital world map with distinct continuity categories and their connected stories, atmospheric depth and selectable regions.

**Hypothesis:** most distinctive feature for returning fans and exploration.  
**Risk:** artistic map should not replace the easy single-watch-order flow, because newcomers need clarity.

## Proposed synthesis (not yet decided)

A could become the editorial home, B the canonical sequence and C a deeper explorer. This avoids forcing every mode onto one crowded screen. The owner should test the prototypes before selecting or blending.

## Art direction review criteria

Compare originality, hierarchy, responsiveness, cinematic immersion, meaningful motion, physical-feeling depth and light, hover/touch behavior, readability, bilingual layout, performance, reduced-motion UX and feature discoverability. Design-led features should have purpose; 'more particles' is not a substitute for composition.

Do not settle for library-default design or generic cards. Research contemporary creative frontends; iterate on the **rendered website**, not merely an abstract description.
